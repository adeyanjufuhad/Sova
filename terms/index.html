01About these terms

By creating an account, signing in or using the demo, you agree to these terms. “Sova”, “we” and “us” mean the people who run Sova, a service built in Nigeria. “You” means the person using it.

Sova is open as a demo for now. Sign-in works through “Try the demo” and a short list of test numbers; we will review these terms before opening to everyone.

02What Sova is, and is not

Sova helps ajo, esusu and adashe circles keep a shared record: who is in the circle, the rules everyone accepted, the payout order, who has paid, what each collector received, and any disputes.

  • Sova is not a bank, lender, wallet, payment service or investment product. It never holds, moves, lends or invests money and does not process payments.
  • Members pay each other directly, by bank transfer or cash, outside Sova.
  • Sova does not guarantee that any member will pay, and it does not chase, collect or refund money.
  • Sova does not check that bank details belong to the person who entered them.

03Who can use Sova

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You need a Nigerian mobile number that you control. One account per person.
  • The name and bank details you give must be your own and correct.

04Your account and PIN

  • Your PIN confirms money actions, such as marking a payment as sent or confirming a payout. Keep it secret. Anything done with your PIN counts as done by you.
  • Sova will never call or text to ask for your PIN or sign-in code.
  • Five wrong PINs lock your account for 15 minutes.
  • If you think someone else knows your PIN, change it in the app and tell us at adeyanjufuhad@gmail.com.

05Circles, rules and payments

  • A circle's rules are agreed between its members. Sova records who accepted which version; it does not enforce the rules outside the app.
  • Each turn's collector receives the contribution from every other member: contribution × (members − 1).
  • The payout order is set by Sova's fair draw once the circle is full. The admin may pledge to collect last, and the draw respects that pledge. Anyone can check the draw on the verify page.
  • Only mark a payment as sent when you have sent it, and only confirm a payment or payout when it has reached you.
  • If a payout is short, the circle still moves to the next turn and a dispute opens automatically against the members who did not pay.
  • Swaps and handovers follow the rules shown in the app and need the other people involved to agree.

06Disputes

When a payment is disputed, other members of the circle can vote on whether it arrived, and the outcome updates the record. A dispute outcome is the circle's view, recorded by Sova. It is not a court decision, and it does not move any money.

Sova does not take sides in disagreements between members. You remain free to settle a disagreement any other way, including through mediation, the police or the courts.

07The record

Every circle has an append-only ledger. Once an entry is written, nobody can edit or delete it, including us, and anyone with the circle's link can check it. Chain heads are also published to a public GitHub branch every 6 hours.

The ledger is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: a change to a past entry shows up when it is checked, but the ledger can't stop someone with full control of the database from trying.

08Sova Score

  • Your Sova Score describes your record of paying your circles. It is your record, not a credit rating, and Sova does not give it to lenders.
  • If you share it, anyone with the link can see the snapshot until you stop sharing. Decisions others make based on your score are theirs, not Sova's.

09Acceptable use

Don't use Sova to:

  • record payments that did not happen, or confirm money you did not receive;
  • pretend to be someone else or use a phone number that isn't yours;
  • deceive, threaten or harass other members;
  • break, overload or get around Sova's security, or collect other people's data from it.

10The demo account

“Try the demo” gives each visit its own private copy of a fictional account and circles, deleted after 24 hours. The demo account's name, bank details and PIN can't be changed. Don't enter real personal or bank information in it.

11Availability and changes

Sova is free to use today. It is new, and parts of it may be slow to wake up, unavailable at times, or change. Features marked “coming soon” or “roadmap” are plans, not promises.

We may change or stop parts of Sova. Where a change affects your circles, we will tell you in the app where we can.

12Ending your use

You can stop using Sova at any time and delete your account in the app once none of your circles is running (see the privacy policy). We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. Either way, entries already in a circle's ledger stay, because other members rely on them.

13Our responsibility

We work to keep Sova accurate and available, but it is provided as it is. Because Sova never handles money, we are not responsible for payments between members, for money a member does not pay, or for losses from relying on what members record.
