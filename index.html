How it works

Start. Contribute. Build trust.

Sova doesn't change how ajo works. It gives it the records and receipts it never had.

  1. 01

    Start your circle

    Set the amount, how often you pay and the group's rules, then share a 6-character invite code. When everyone has joined, Sova draws the payout order fairly.

  2. 02

    Everyone contributes

    Members pay each other the way they already do, then mark it in Sova. The collector confirms it arrived, so no one argues about who paid.

  3. 03

    Build your record

    Every payment you make on time builds your Sova record: a history of keeping your word that belongs to you.

Features

Everything your circle argues about, settled.

Built around the real reasons ajo groups break: forgotten payments, disputed records, emergencies mid-cycle and people who collect early and disappear.

A receipt both sides confirm

The payer marks the money as sent; the collector confirms it arrived with their PIN. Both get the same receipt, so “I paid” and “I never got it” can't both be true.

Group rules everyone signs

Late fines, leaving early, sickness and emergencies agreed up front. Sova saves who accepted which version, and when.

Proof of payment

Record the bank reference and attach a photo of the receipt. Only your circle can see it, and the collector confirms with their PIN.

Members vouch for newcomers

A new member joins on someone's word. If they collect and stop paying, the group sees it on the record.

Disputes the circle decides

A short payout opens a dispute automatically. If a payment is disputed, the members not involved vote on whether the money arrived, and every vote goes on the record.

Our promise

Trust is the whole product.

An ajo only works when everyone believes the record. These are the rules Sova is built on, and they don't change.

What Sova is not

  • Not a bank
  • Not a lender
  • Not a wallet
  1. 01

    We never hold your money

    Contributions go straight from member to member, through your own bank or in cash. There is no Sova wallet to empty.

  2. 02

    Your record belongs to you

    Your payment history is yours. It is only shared with a lender, landlord or supplier when you choose to share it.

  3. 03

    We do not sell your data

    Your name, number and payments are used to run your circle and nothing else. Sova is built to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

  4. 04

    Every naira is accounted for

    Both sides confirm each payment, short payouts open a dispute on their own, and disputes are settled by the record, not by memory.
